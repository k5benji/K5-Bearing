Three faiths. Eight branches.

Each branch keeps its own calendar and its own hours, all of it worked out for where you are. Whichever you follow is the one the app is built around.

  • Karaite
  • Conservative / Masorti

Christianity

  • Orthodox
  • Catholic
  • Protestant

Each branch’s year, fasts, and Daily Office stand on their own calendar mathematics.

Islam

  • Sunni
  • Shia / Twelver

Prayer windows and Qibla are calculated per school, never approximated from a single source.

Five spaces. Built for prayer.

When to pray, what to read, and where the year stands.

  • Today — Next prayer and the current window. What today is in your own calendar: ordinary, a fast, or a day set apart.
  • Prayer — The services, the readings, the psalms and the prayers appointed for the day, in the original script alongside the English. A guided washing flow when your branch asks for one.
  • Calendar — The branch’s own year with its dates — never forced into a single generic calendar.
  • Active — Guided sessions, a daily ring, streaks, and mercy days. Progress that stays gentle and never demands more than you can give.
  • Profile — The branch you follow, lifetime totals, and a year of days at a glance. Arrange it as you like. It syncs through your own iCloud.

Also included: a compass for your branch’s direction · Live Activity countdown · Home Screen widget · Reminders built per branch.

Offline-first. Private by default.

Prayer times and the sun and moon calculations your calendar turns on live entirely on your device. You sign in with Apple, and the account that creates lives with Apple rather than with us.

  • Local solar & lunar math — no network needed for times
  • Sign in with Apple. We never see your password
  • Progress syncs only through your personal iCloud
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking
